[ SECURITY ]

Security, stated plainly.

What HR & Payroll does to protect people's data today, what depends on your installation, and what we don't claim.

[ IN THE PRODUCT ]

Controls built into HR & Payroll.

  • In the product

    Sign-in and sessions

    Short-lived access tokens; the web app keeps its session in a secure, HttpOnly cookie. Account lockout after repeated failed sign-ins, idle sign-out and a configurable password policy.

  • In the product

    Two-factor and single sign-on

    Authenticator-app (TOTP) two-factor authentication, which administrators can require for everyone. Single sign-on through OpenID Connect; LDAP / Active Directory for on-premises installs.

  • In the product

    Role-based access with record scopes

    Roles are built from fine-grained permissions, and scoped to branches or departments. Companies are isolated from each other, and every service checks access itself.

  • In the product

    Separation of duties

    Payroll moves through calculate, submit, review, approve and finalize with separate permissions; nobody approves their own leave, overtime or attendance correction.

  • In the product

    Audit trail

    Changes to people, pay, access and settings are recorded by an audit service, and can be exported to your SIEM. Log searches and exports are audited too.

  • In the product

    Files encrypted and scanned

    Documents are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and scanned for viruses (ClamAV) before they are accepted. Bank details, device credentials and SSO secrets are encrypted as well.

  • In the product

    Personal data kept out of logs

    Emails and long numbers (government IDs, phone and account numbers) are masked before a log line is written, and again when logs are read.

  • In the product

    Hardened edge

    One API gateway in front of every service: request signing between gateway and services, rate limiting, request size limits, security headers and an optional admin IP allowlist.

  • In the product

    Privacy requests

    Employees can file privacy requests (access, correction, objection and others) that a privacy officer tracks to completion.

  • In the product

    Monitoring

    The Operations Center checks every service and data store, raises alerts and records maintenance windows.

  • Depends on your install

    Production configuration

    Some protections depend on the installation: TLS at the web server, secured Kafka and object-storage credentials, and your backup routine. The installation guide marks each one.

[ ACCESS CONTROL ]

Roles that match how HR and payroll divide work.

Built-in roles — HR manager, HR officer, payroll officer, payroll approver, line manager, auditor and more — are made of individual permissions. Each user's access can be limited to a branch or department.

[ WHAT WE DON'T CLAIM ]

No borrowed badges.

We'd rather tell you what isn't there than let you assume it is.

  • No security certification (such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2) — we don't claim one.
  • No third-party penetration test has been published yet.
  • Compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) depends on how each company uses and configures the system; we don't claim it on your behalf.
  • Statutory payroll rules should be reviewed by your accountant against current rates before going live.

[ THIS WEBSITE ]

And this site?

  • Served over HTTPS with a strict Content Security Policy and security headers.
  • No advertising or tracking cookies; privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics you can switch off.
  • Contact messages are validated, rate-limited and only emailed to the team — not stored in a database.
  • Product screenshots use a fictional company; no real employee or payroll data appears on this site.
