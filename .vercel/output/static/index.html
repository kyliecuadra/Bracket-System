[ HOW PAYROLL MOVES ]

Four eyes on every payroll run.

Each step has its own permission, so the person who prepares a run isn't the person who approves it.

  1. 01

    Calculate

    Payroll officer

    Pay, premiums, deductions and contributions from the attendance, leave and loans already in the system.

  2. 02

    Submit

    Payroll officer

    The run is locked for review, with totals for gross, deductions, net pay and employer contributions.

  3. 03

    Review & approve

    Payroll approver

    A different person reviews and approves — or sends it back with a reason.

  4. 04

    Finalize

    Payroll approver

    Payslips appear in self-service and loans are applied. Corrections go through a linked reversal run.

[ SECURITY & RELIABILITY ]

Built for data people trust you with.

Payroll and HR records are some of the most sensitive data a company holds. These controls are in the product today — and we're clear about the ones that depend on your installation.

Security overview

  • Sign-in and sessions

    Short-lived access tokens; the web app keeps its session in a secure, HttpOnly cookie. Account lockout after repeated failed sign-ins, idle sign-out and a configurable password policy.

  • Two-factor and single sign-on

    Authenticator-app (TOTP) two-factor authentication, which administrators can require for everyone. Single sign-on through OpenID Connect; LDAP / Active Directory for on-premises installs.

  • Role-based access with record scopes

    Roles are built from fine-grained permissions, and scoped to branches or departments. Companies are isolated from each other, and every service checks access itself.

  • Separation of duties

    Payroll moves through calculate, submit, review, approve and finalize with separate permissions; nobody approves their own leave, overtime or attendance correction.

  • Audit trail

    Changes to people, pay, access and settings are recorded by an audit service, and can be exported to your SIEM. Log searches and exports are audited too.

  • Files encrypted and scanned

    Documents are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and scanned for viruses (ClamAV) before they are accepted. Bank details, device credentials and SSO secrets are encrypted as well.
